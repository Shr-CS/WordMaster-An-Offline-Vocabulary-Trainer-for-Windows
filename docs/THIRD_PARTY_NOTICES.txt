WordMaster vocabulary data — sources and attribution

1. ECDICT — https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT
Revision 39cfb9672c0906b63569e0453c2e6abfb9ab182a. English-Chinese senses, word forms,
frequency references and exam tags. Adapted: specialist markup removed, glosses split
by part of speech, inflected entries merged where appropriate, reviewed corrections.
MIT License. Copyright (c) 2025 Linwei. Full license follows below.

2. ipa-dict — https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict
Revision 972cd67dd5f96bc5660e28070cde26e3928abcd7, en_US only.
English US IPA based on cmudict-ipa by @lingz and syllabify by @kylebgorman.
Copyright (c) 2016 dohliam, MIT. Converted /ɹ/ and /ɫ/ to dictionary /r/ and /l/.
ECDICT pronunciation is used as a labeled British fallback; editorial corrections
are recorded separately. No UK ipa-dict data is included.

3. CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5 — compiled by Yukio Tono,
Tokyo University of Foreign Studies (Tono Laboratory).
https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj
Revision d4e45b75b38f27b30dfc5c44d8c571aec7e7092f.
Retrieved by Open Language Profiles from http://www.cefr-j.org/download.html
on 2020-01-20. Used as a reference for the A1 bank and CEFR/part-of-speech metadata.
Copyright belongs to Tono Laboratory, TUFS. Research and commercial use is free
with proper citation. Neither CEFR-J nor Open Language Profiles warrants accuracy.
The A1 reference bank is an adaptation and is not a Chinese school curriculum.
No Octanove C1/C2 data is included.

4. Tatoeba contributors — https://tatoeba.org
Sentence pair selection by Charles Kelly at https://www.manythings.org/anki/
Snapshot 2026-02-13, cmn-eng.zip. CC BY 2.0 France:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
Sentences are selected by length and lexical alignment, retained verbatim.
Each included pair carries its English/Chinese sentence IDs, contributor names,
original attribution and source URL in src/data/examples.json and in the app.
Not every word has a corpus example. No audio data is redistributed.
